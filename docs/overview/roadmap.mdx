## Now: testnet (September – October 2026)

Built during Monad Metropolis.

- [x] Credit line contract: modular UUPS proxy, lender pool, collateral in a yield vault, card hold, one identity per account, score and limit, defaults. Verified on Monad testnet
- [x] Over 100 Foundry tests at 100% coverage, invariant tests, Slither in CI
- [x] Gasless sends (ERC-3009) and one-transaction repayments (permit)
- [x] Envio indexer: history, public record page, daily reconciliation
- [x] Backend live: KYC with Didit, payments with Xendit, relayer, API with Swagger
- [x] Ringgit in through Xendit Malaysia (FPX, DuitNow), rupiah in and out through Xendit Indonesia
- [x] Landing site
- [ ] Passkey app (PWA)
- [ ] User testing with at least five Indonesian workers in Malaysia and their families

## Next: mainnet

- **Real yield:** move collateral from the testnet mock to earnAUSD.
- **Issuer-side fiat:** Agora's Routes API to mint and redeem AUSD directly against fiat, removing Matocard's own treasury float.
- **Governance:** the admin key behind a multisig and a timelock long enough for users to repay and leave.
- **Third-party audit** of the contracts.
- **Licensed partners** for the card and the remittance legs.

## Later

- **A real card:** a physical and virtual card through a licensed issuer. Today the card number is visual only.
- **Passkey recovery:** rebind a verified identity to a new account after a waiting period.
- **A credit passport:** the record read directly by partner banks and other protocols.
- **More corridors** beyond Malaysia → Indonesia.
- **Native app and WhatsApp notifications.**
- **Collateral options:** rebalancing across vaults, collateral from other chains.

## Regulation

Credit and a card need a licensed bank or issuer. Remittance needs a Bank Indonesia licence and Malaysian approval. Crypto assets in Indonesia are supervised by OJK. The realistic path is licensed partners, or OJK's regulatory sandbox (POJK 3/2024). Matocard is a testnet product until then.
