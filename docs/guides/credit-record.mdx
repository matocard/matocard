Every Matocard account has a public record at `/verify/<account>`. Share the link with anyone who needs to check your history: a lender, a landlord, a bank back home.

## What it shows

| | |
|---|---|
| Score | 0 to 100 |
| Collateral ratio | What a new limit would need |
| Cycles | How many, how many repaid, each with its dates, peak and outcome |
| Proof | A link to the opening and closing transaction of every cycle on the Monad explorer |
| Status | Verified, and whether the account has ever defaulted |

## What it never shows

Your name, your document number, your balance, or who you sent money to.

## Why it can be trusted

The page reads the contract directly for the score and counts, and the [indexer](/developers/indexer) for the history. Each cycle links to its transactions, so the reader does not need to trust Matocard: they can check every figure on the explorer themselves.
