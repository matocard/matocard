---
title: "Cash out"
description: "Turn AUSD into rupiah in an Indonesian bank account."
icon: "building-columns"
---

<Steps>
  <Step title="Tap Cash out">
    Enter the amount in AUSD or rupiah. The app shows what will reach the bank at a rate locked for 60 seconds. At least Rp 10,000.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the bank account">
    Bank, account number and the account holder's name, exactly as the bank has it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    You sign once. The AUSD moves to Matocard, then Xendit pays the rupiah to the bank.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Track it">
    The payout shows as **in progress** in your history until the bank confirms it.
  </Step>
</Steps>
