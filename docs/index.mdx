## The problem

Millions of Indonesians work abroad, and Malaysia is one of the largest destinations. A worker like **Siti**, 29, in Kuala Lumpur, sends money to her mother in Yogyakarta every month. She has a debit card and a phone. What she does not have is a credit history:

- **Not in Malaysia.** She is a foreign worker with no local record, so banks treat her as a stranger.
- **Not in Indonesia either.** She left before she ever borrowed.
- **And it would not travel.** A record built with a Malaysian bank stays with that bank, in that country, when she moves home.

When cash runs short before payday, what is left are predatory online lenders and loan sharks.

## The solution

Matocard is an **interest-free credit line backed by your own savings**, whose limit grows every time you settle on time, and whose record lives on a public blockchain that you own.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Deposit once, borrow against it" icon="piggy-bank">
    Top up by card or bank transfer. The money becomes collateral and earns yield while it sits there.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Settle on time, get more" icon="chart-line">
    Each cycle repaid on time raises your score, and the score lowers the collateral needed: from 150% for a new account down to 80%.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Send home instantly" icon="paper-plane">
    Borrow straight to your family's Matocard. It arrives in under a second, and they can cash out to their bank in rupiah.
  </Card>
  <Card title="A record that moves with you" icon="earth-asia">
    Your score and history live on Monad. Anyone can verify them, nobody can edit them, and they still count when you move home.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

No crypto wallet, no seed phrase, no gas to buy. You sign up with your fingerprint or Face ID, and the blockchain stays out of sight.

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Network | Monad testnet (chain `10143`) |
| Unit of account | [AUSD](https://www.agora.finance) by Agora, a US dollar stablecoin |
| Money in | Ringgit (FPX, DuitNow QR, cards) and rupiah (virtual accounts, QRIS, cards) through Xendit |
| Money out | Rupiah to Indonesian banks through Xendit |
| Identity | Didit: ID document and selfie, one identity per account |
| Interest | None. The protocol keeps 20% of the yield your collateral earns |
| Status | Contracts, backend and indexer live on testnet; the app is in progress |
