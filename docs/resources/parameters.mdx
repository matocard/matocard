## Contract

| Parameter | Testnet | Production | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| `term` | 30 days | 30 days | Time from a cycle's first draw to its due date |
| `grace` | 3 days | 3 days | Extra time before a cycle can be defaulted |
| `minCycleDuration` | 60 s | 7 days | Shortest cycle that counts toward the score |
| `cardHold` | 60 s | 7 days | How long a card top-up waits before counting |
| `minUtilizationBps` | 1000 (10%) | 1000 (10%) | Smallest peak use of the limit that counts |
| `yieldFeeBps` | 2000 (20%) | 2000 (20%) | Share of collateral yield kept for lenders |

The collateral ratio is not a parameter: it is `15000 − 7000 × score / 100` basis points, in the `CreditScoring` library.
